C — Monochrome + Contrast Pops

Nearly black and white with surgical color only where it matters — a green "live" badge, a light card that demands attention. Thin/bold font weight contrast instead of color contrast. Project cards flip between light and dark. Says "I let the work speak for itself."

Note: These are aesthetic directions, not final designs. The winner gets fully developed with proper typography, animations, layout structure, and component design. You can also mix — e.g., "A's warmth with B's layout clarity."
